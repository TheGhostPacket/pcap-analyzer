Detection Capabilities

What it finds in your captures

Port Scanning
Detects both vertical scans (many ports, one host) and horizontal scans (one port, many hosts) via SYN packet analysis.
ARP Spoofing
Identifies conflicting IP-to-MAC mappings in ARP replies — the primary indicator of Man-in-the-Middle attacks.
DNS Tunneling
Flags long subdomains, high Shannon entropy queries, and excessive unique DNS lookups — common data exfiltration techniques.
Brute Force Attacks
Detects credential stuffing and brute force patterns against SSH (22), FTP (21), and HTTP/HTTPS services.
Suspicious Ports
Flags traffic on known malicious ports — Metasploit (4444), Tor (9001/9050), IRC botnet C2 (6666/6667), and others.
ICMP Floods & Anomalies
Detects high-volume ICMP echo requests indicative of ping flood DoS attacks, plus large data transfer anomalies.
How It Works

Three steps to a threat report

1
Upload your PCAP
Drag and drop or browse for a .pcap, .pcapng, or .cap file. Captured with Wireshark, tcpdump, or any standard tool. Max 50MB.
2
Scapy parses every packet
Seven independent detection modules run against your capture — each using real protocol-level heuristics, not pattern matching against known signatures.
3
Get a prioritised report
Findings are ranked Critical → Low with full descriptions, affected IPs, and packet counts. Export the full report as CSV for documentation.
